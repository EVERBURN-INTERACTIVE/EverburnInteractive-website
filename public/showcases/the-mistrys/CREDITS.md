# Texture credits

Concept demo for The Mistrys. Fabric and wood images are example finishes, not The Mistrys' catalogue.

All textures are Poly Haven assets under the CC0 license (https://polyhaven.com/license). Diffuse maps were downloaded as 1K JPEG, tiled, and saved as JPEG. The default velvet and teak materials also use those assets' `nor_gl` and `Rough` maps.
